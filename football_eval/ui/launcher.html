1Setup~10 min, once per match

Pitch size, periods, direction of play, overlays, kit clicks, landmarks and the match sheet — what one camera cannot infer reliably. Opens in Manual decisions.

2Process the video~1-2 h for a full match (standard, laptop)

Detection, tracking, teams, camera, ball → events, xG, stats, opportunities and the report. Clips of the flagged opportunities are cut at the end. Watch it work on the Match screen (Live).

3Review~10 min

Check ~16 frames and every goal / shot (confirm, reject, flip team, shirt number, shot location), repair lost camera stretches, add missing events, exclude stretches.

4Apply the reviewseconds

Re-runs only the analytics with your verdicts: stats, opportunities, report.
