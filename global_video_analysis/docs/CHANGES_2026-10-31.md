# Changes 2026-10-31 — replay, ball, attacks near goal

Feedback on the full Canedo 1080p run (camera usable on 88 % of play, ball in 10 %): live processing is right, but
the **replay** shows uncoloured boxes and wrong pitch lines; the ball should be the priority because there are few
events, and the goal is to find **offensive play near the goals**.

## 2 · Ball (`football_eval/balltrack.py`, `ball.detector: tracker` — new default)

The 1080p frame is cut into ~1024-px tiles that the network sees at 640 px: an 8–12 px ball becomes 5–7 px, below
what the COCO detector fires on. The tracker of the simple pipeline (`simple/ball.py`, ~45 % of frames on J3) is now
in the main pipeline, adapted to the 10-fps grid and the PTZ camera:

* Kalman filter moved with the PTZ image motion between analysed frames (a pan is not a ball jump);
* zoomed re-detection around the prediction (320-px window ×2 → the ball becomes ~20 px);
* motion-compensated moving bright blobs (at 640 px) as weak candidates;
* while no ball is tracked, a native-resolution scan of the grass band every 2 analysed frames;
* static-object filter (2 s still, nobody near), on-grass / not-on-a-shirt rules; full-frame hits down to 0.05.

Measured on 30 s of the clip (60–90 s, 300 analysed frames): measured ball frames **62 → 73** (+18 %). Honest
assessment: in deep shade and at the far end the COCO detector itself is the limit — a ball detector trained on this
venue (we can now generate the training labels from the tracker + your review) is the next real step.

## 3 · Attacks near goal (`football_eval/pressure.py`, rulebook §3.8) — offensive play without the ball

Play position per frame = the ball when seen, else the centre of the outfield players in view (the operator follows
the play); a phase = ≥ 3 s within 25 m of a goal with the ball there or ≥ 3 players in the box, and the attacking team
present. Each phase is an `attack_near_goal` event: timeline, replay banner (whole phase), *Key events* list,
opportunities (*Attack near goal*, or build-up of a shot / set piece of the same team), team stats
(`attacks_near_goal`, `time_near_opp_goal_min`).

On the 4-min clip: 4 phases — Canedo at the left goal 0:39 (10 s, 12 in the box) and 1:34 (11 s), Mansores at the
right goal 3:00 and 3:49 — all four checked on the frames.
